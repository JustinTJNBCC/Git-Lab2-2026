## Overview

This guide provides common troubleshooting steps for wireless connectivity issues.

## Unable to Connect to Wi‑Fi

1. Verify that Wi‑Fi is enabled on the device.
2. Confirm that Airplane Mode is disabled.
3. Ensure the correct wireless network is selected.
4. Restart the device.
5. Attempt to reconnect.

## Known Issues

No issues currently documented.

## Escalation

If the issue persists after all troubleshooting steps have been completed, contact the Service Desk.

## Slow Wireless Connection

1. Make sure you are close to the wifi
2. Make sure you are actually connected to the wifi you think you are connected to
3. Restart you system to try resolving the issue
